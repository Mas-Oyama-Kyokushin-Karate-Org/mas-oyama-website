Our Achievements

AUGUST 2026

Open Independence Cup

Location: Madhyamgram

Achievement: 15/15 position holders

We participated in the Open Independence Cup held at Madhyamgram in August 2026, organized by the Bengal Kyokushin Karate Organization, North 24 Parganas. All 15 of our participants secured positions in their respective categories. Our students also won the Open Category Heavyweight and Middleweight finals for boys, as well as the Heavyweight final for girls.

DECEMBER 2025

All India Open Invitation Kyokushin Karate Championship

Organizer: Shymnagar United Kyokushin Karate Organization

Location: Boral,Garia

Achievement: All participants won in their respective categories. Boys' Open Heavyweight — 2nd and 3rd place. Girls' Open Heavyweight — Champion.

We participated in the All India Open Invitation Kyokushin Karate Championship held in December 2025, organized by Shymnagar United Kyokushin Karate Organization. All our participants won in their respective categories. Our students secured 2nd and 3rd place in the Boys' Open Heavyweight category, while our student became champion in the Girls' Open Heavyweight category.

2024

3rd All India Open Kyokushin Karate Tournament – Kyokushin Youth Cup

Location: Dakshindari

Achievement: All participants won in their respective categories, with some securing two championship titles in Kata and Kumite.

We participated in the 3rd All India Open Kyokushin Karate Tournament – Kyokushin Youth Cup, held at Dakshindari in 2024. All our participants won in their respective categories. Some of our students also secured two championship titles, winning both the Kata and Kumite events.
