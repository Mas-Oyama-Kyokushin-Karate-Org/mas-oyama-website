Welcome to Our Dojo

Traditional Kyokushin Karate

Welcome to the official website of the Masutatsu Oyama Kyokushin Karate Organization, based in Tangra, Kolkata, West Bengal.

Our dojo is dedicated to the practice and teaching of Kyokushin Karate, emphasizing discipline, perseverance, physical fitness, technical ability and personal development.

Through regular training, examinations, competitions and special events, students are encouraged to develop both their karate skills and character.

OUR SENSEI

Meet Our Sensei

Mr. Satyeswar Mandal

4th Dan Black Belt (YonDan)

Sensei Satyeswar Mandal has been the Dojo-in-Charge of the Masutatsu Oyama Kyokushin Karate Organization since 1994.

He was awarded the honorary 4th Dan Black Belt (YonDan) in 2024. He also serves as the Branch Chief of the Kolkata Branch of IKO Matsushima, Japan.
