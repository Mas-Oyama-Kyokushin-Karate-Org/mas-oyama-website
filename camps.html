Our Summer Camps

2026

Summer Camp 2026

Summer Camp 2026 brought four dojos together in Puri, Odisha, for a disciplined regimen of three daily training sessions starting at dawn.

In addition to regular training, the camp empowered senior brown belts and junior black belts through specialized judge training.

2025

Summer Camp 2025

Set against the scenic hills of Darjeeling, West Bengal, the 5-day Summer Camp 2025 offered participants an ideal blend of high-altitude training and breathtaking natural beauty.

2024

Summer Camp 2024

Summer Camp 2024 took place over five days in Digha, West Bengal, uniting four different dojos for an immersive joint training experience.

On the fifth day, participants took on the challenge of strictly supervised open-sea training. As waves washed over them, the sheer thrill of training in the ocean eclipsed the hardship, turning a tough session into an unforgettable experience.
