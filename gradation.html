GRADATION SYSTEM

The Path of Progression

The gradation system follows a progressive belt structure beginning with the White Belt and continuing through Orange, Blue, Yellow, Green and Brown grades before reaching the Black Belt Shodan level.

From the Blue Belt onward, each belt has a corresponding Kyu grade. The number of opponents to face increases at every stage of progression. Starting with one opponent for the first promotion, the requirement increases progressively up to ten opponents for the final progression from Brown Kyu to Black Belt Shodan.
